Welcome to B3D

Custom 3D printing services for teachers, students, and creators looking to bring digital blueprints into the real world. Any open design file found on MakerWorld can be scaled and printed.

The Print Setup & Color Inventory

Prints are made using high-speed Bambu Lab P2S setups, known for delivering smooth surfaces and highly consistent structural quality. Paired with an automated material system, creating seamless multi-color projects happens right out of the box without messy manual filament changes.

Material selections focus on delivering excellent variety tailored to specific projects:

  • Over 30 Different Colors: A massive on-hand stock selection featuring deep primary colors, soft pastel layouts, and neon highlights.
  • Multi-Color Customization: Designs can combine up to 4 different colors within a single print layer, making logos, text elements, and artwork stand out clearly.
  • Smooth Wall Finishes: Advanced mechanical flow calibration ensures that layer lines disappear, leaving a clean, highly uniform surface texture.

End of Year Name Tags

Finding a unique, meaningful gift for an entire classroom or school department at the end of the term can be challenging. Custom 3D-printed name tags offer a fun, lasting keepsake that students and staff love attaching to school bags.

These customized gifts focus on clean layouts and simple personalization options:

  • Classroom Lists: Simply supply a list of student names, and individual tags can be generated and printed for everybody.
  • Color Selections: Multiple filament color choices are available, allowing for bright, high-contrast layouts that stand out.
  • Durable Finishes: The plastic is tough and lightweight, built to survive daily school backpack wear and tear over summer holidays and beyond.

Teaching Tools for All Year Groups

Lessons become much more engaging when abstract ideas transform into physical objects that can be passed around the room. Custom-made learning aids provide teachers across all year groups with an affordable way to add tactile, interactive elements to daily lessons.

Popular prints for educational setups include:

  • Counting & Math Visuals: Interactive counting tools, base ten blocks, colorful geometric shapes, fraction plates that click together, and tactile puzzles for hands-on problem solving.
  • Classroom Upgrades: Practical organizing trays, sturdy whiteboard marker holders, or custom desk hooks to keep learning spaces tidy.

Decorative Models & Fair Pricing

Beyond classroom equipment, custom printing covers a massive range of decorative ornaments, desktop displays, and useful gadgets for shelves, study spaces, or home offices. It is clear that almost any model on MakerWorld can be printed by B3D.

Pricing remains completely fair, open, and clear:

  • Dedicated Pricing Calculator: Project costs are determined using a dedicated 3D printing price calculator. This system factors in precise filament consumption alongside a safety margin for print failures, ensuring accurate and balanced quotes.
  • MakerWorld Integration: The MakerWorld platform is packed with incredible models created by community designers. Simply find an open design file, share the link, and the physical print will be handled from start to finish.
