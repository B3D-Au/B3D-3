Welcome to B3D

Custom 3D printing services for teachers, students, and creators looking to bring digital blueprints to life. Any open file found on MakerWorld can be scaled, printed, and delivered locally.

The Print Setup & Color Inventory

Prints are run using high-speed Bambu Lab P2S setups, known for delivering smooth surfaces and highly consistent structural quality. Paired with an automated material system, creating seamless multi-color projects happens right out of the box without messy manual filament changes.

Material selections focus on premium, long-lasting options tailored to specific needs:

  • Over 30 Different Colors: A massive on-hand selection featuring deep primary colors, soft pastel layouts, neon highlights, and translucent variations.
  • Multi-Color Customization: Designs can combine up to 4 different colors within a single print layer, making logos, text elements, and artwork stand out clearly.
  • Smooth Wall Finishes: Advanced mechanical flow calibration ensures that layer lines disappear, leaving a clean, highly professional surface texture.

End of Year Name Tags

Finding a unique, meaningful gift for an entire classroom or school department at the end of the term can be challenging. Custom 3D-printed name tags offer a fun, lasting keepsake that students and staff love attaching to school bags, keys, or desks.

These customized gifts focus on clean layouts and simple personalization options:

  • Classroom Lists: Simply supply a list of student names, and individual, custom-made tags can be generated for every person.
  • Color Selections: Multiple filament color choices are available, allowing for bright, high-contrast layouts that stand out.
  • Durable Finishes: The plastic is tough and lightweight, built to survive daily school backpack wear and tear over summer holidays and beyond.

Teaching Tools

Lessons become much more engaging when abstract ideas transform into physical objects that can be passed around the room. Custom-made learning aids provide teachers with an affordable way to add tactile, interactive elements to daily lessons.

Popular prints for educational setups include:

  • Mathematics Visuals: Colorful geometric blocks, fraction plates that click together, and tactile puzzles for problem-solving.
  • Science and History Blueprints: Scaled molecular structures, cell diagrams, or detailed architectural models for history discussions.
  • Classroom Upgrades: Practical organizing trays, sturdy whiteboard marker holders, or custom desk hooks to keep learning spaces tidy.

Decorative Models

Beyond classroom equipment, custom printing covers a massive range of decorative ornaments, desktop displays, and useful gadgets for shelves, study spaces, or home offices.

Accessing the massive community library of 3D designs is straightforward:

  • MakerWorld Blueprints: The MakerWorld platform is packed with incredible models created by community designers, including articulated animals, complex geometric sculptures, and display pieces.
  • Zero Technical Knowledge Needed: There is no need to worry about complex slicing software or printer calibrations. Simply find an open design file on MakerWorld, share the link, and the physical print will be handled from start to finish.
